# Finow content and compliance guardrails

Finow content may touch finance and health. Apply higher evidence standards.

## Financial claims

Use only verified current product facts or official provider/Finow sources for:

- credit limits
- fees
- installment counts
- repayment terms
- settlement timing
- eligibility
- provider availability

If verification is missing, write a dependency instead of a number.

## Health claims

Do not create medical diagnosis, treatment promises or efficacy claims from SEO inference.

When health content needs a factual medical assertion, prefer authoritative medical/health sources and distinguish general information from individual medical advice.

## Reviews and testimonials

Never generate a fake review. Preserve supplied reviews accurately except for clearly disclosed spelling cleanup that does not change meaning.

## Partnerships and logos

Do not infer an active partnership from an old page, logo file or mention. Verify current status before presenting it as active.
