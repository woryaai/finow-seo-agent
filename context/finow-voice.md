# Finow voice

## B2C voice

Warm, calm, approachable, modern, optimistic and trustworthy.

Use simple Persian. Explain financial terms in plain language. Avoid dry banking language and exaggerated promises.

## B2B voice

Professional, concise, confident, data-aware and growth-oriented.

Lead with business value, operational clarity and verified proof. Avoid hype.

## Global rules

- No forced humor cadence.
- No fabricated urgency.
- No invented scarcity.
- No unsupported superlatives.
- Do not call a medical patient a generic «مشتری» when «مراجعه‌کننده» or «بیمار» is contextually correct.
- Use approved Persian writing rules from `references/persian-writing.md`.
