# Finow brand context

**What this is:** stable brand positioning used across SEO and content.  
**Status:** active.  
**Next action:** use this context unless an approved brand document overrides it.

## Brand

- Name: Finow
- Persian name: فاینو
- Brand line: «فاینو، حالِ خوبِ من»
- Core domains: سلامت، زیبایی، تندرستی، دندان‌پزشکی

## Brand role

Finow connects consumers and eligible merchants with financial/payment and business-growth capabilities relevant to health, beauty, wellness and dental services.

For B2B content, Finow may be positioned as a revenue-growth platform when the specific capabilities mentioned are supported by verified product facts.

## Visual defaults

- Persian RTL
- Kalameh FaNum when the site design system supports it
- Crimson-led identity
- generous white space
- concise hierarchy
- mobile-first readability

Do not redesign an existing production design system merely to satisfy an SEO task.
