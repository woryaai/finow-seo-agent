# Finow audiences

## B2C

People seeking health, beauty, wellness or dental services and evaluating whether they can pay using credit/installments.

Common needs:

- understand eligibility and payment flow
- discover services/merchants
- compare payment options
- know real fees, limits and repayment terms
- trust that terms are current

Primary outcomes:

- registration
- credit/eligibility check
- merchant/service discovery
- booking
- transaction initiation

## B2B

Clinics, dentists, doctors, salons, wellness centers and related merchants.

Common needs:

- increase qualified demand and conversion
- accept credit/installment payments
- understand settlement
- activate quickly
- manage relationships, campaigns and retention where supported

Primary outcomes:

- merchant signup
- qualified lead
- onboarding
- activation
- first transaction
