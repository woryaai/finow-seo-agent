# Persian writing and RTL rules

## Direction

- Persian text is RTL and right-aligned in UI layouts.
- Lists, bullets and icons begin from the right for Persian sections.
- Form labels, placeholders, help text and errors are RTL.
- Mixed Persian/English fragments must not break visual direction.

## Typography

- Prefer Kalameh FaNum when compatible with the site design system.
- Use Persian «ی» and «ک».
- Use correct half-spaces such as «می‌شود»، «پرداخت‌های»، «ثبت‌نام».
- Prefer Persian numerals in Persian marketing/UI copy unless technical identifiers require Latin digits.

## Editorial style

- Use Persian punctuation.
- Keep headings concise.
- Avoid literal English translations when natural Persian exists.
- Prefer «تومان» for approved consumer pricing in Iran unless the source explicitly uses another unit.
- For medical contexts, prefer «مراجعه‌کننده» or «بیمار» where appropriate instead of generic «مشتری».

## SEO titles and metadata

Clarity and search intent come before keyword repetition. Do not produce unnatural keyword-stuffed Persian.
