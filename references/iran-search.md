# Iran-first search intelligence

## Principle

Do not fabricate Iranian keyword volume/difficulty to make a keyword map look complete.

## Evidence ladder

1. **Google Search Console:** actual impressions, clicks, CTR, average position and query/page relationships.
2. **Live Persian SERP:** page types, titles, ranking domains, ads, local/transactional signals, forum/video presence and result diversity.
3. **Autocomplete / related searches / recurring question patterns:** useful for vocabulary and long-tail discovery, not precise volume.
4. **Google Trends:** direction and seasonality, not absolute search volume.
5. **Competitor evidence:** ranking pages, information architecture and keyword/page overlap.
6. **Third-party tools:** only when the market/database coverage is valid. Label estimates.

## Demand labels

When exact trustworthy volume is absent, use one of:

- `High observed demand`
- `Moderate observed demand`
- `Emerging/seasonal`
- `Low but high-intent`
- `Unknown - needs GSC/tool data`

Document the evidence instead of inventing a monthly number.

## Intent classification

Classify from the live SERP, not just from words in the query:

- Transactional
- Commercial investigation
- Informational
- Navigational
- Local
- Mixed

Record the dominant result type and any important SERP features.

## Opportunity scoring

A Finow opportunity score may combine:

- business value
- intent strength
- observed demand
- competition
- existing Finow visibility
- click potential
- content/product fit

This score is an internal prioritization heuristic, not a Google metric.
