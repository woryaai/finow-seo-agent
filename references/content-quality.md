# Content quality gate

Every new/rewritten page must score at least 9/10 before it can become `Ready`.

Score one point each for:

1. Search intent satisfaction
2. Audience clarity
3. Factual trust/evidence
4. Useful originality or Finow-specific value
5. Clear structure
6. Brand voice fit
7. Strong but accurate CTA
8. Mobile readability
9. SEO completeness
10. No stale/unverified commercial claims

A 10/10 content-quality score is not a promise of ranking. Performance data after publication remains the real outcome test.
